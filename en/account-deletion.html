Deleting in the app

  • Open Patanapló and tap the cog at the top right.
  • Find the Account and cloud backup section.
  • On the Cloud backup card, press Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account and your data stored in the cloud (horses, diaries, costs, documents, photos) immediately; for your entries in horses shared with you by others, see below. The data on your phone remains; you can delete it with the "Delete all data and start again" button under cog → Data → Advanced, or by uninstalling the app.

Deleting by email

If you no longer have the app, write to kapcsolat.elteto@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Patanapló account deletion". We delete the account and its data within 30 days (for shared horses see below) and confirm it.

Deleting some data (keeping the account)

You do not have to delete your account to delete some of your data:

  • In the app: any entry (task, session, health entry, cost, document, photo, message, contact) can be deleted with the Delete button in the card's "⋯" menu. You can delete one of your own horses with all its data using the "[horse name] – delete" button at the bottom of the Profile section of the Horse & team tab. You cannot delete a horse shared with you, but you can leave it with the "Leave the shared horse" button in the Team section. If you are signed in, the deletion also happens in the cloud at the next backup.
  • By email: write to kapcsolat.elteto@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, and tell us which data to delete (e.g. a horse's data, the photos or the documents). We delete it within 30 days and confirm it.

Deleted data disappears permanently from the provider's backups within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted, and what remains?

Deleted: the account (email address, identifier) and all your own data and files stored in the cloud (for shared horses see below). It disappears permanently from the provider's backups within 30 days at the latest.

What remains: your entries in horses shared with you by others stay with the horse (see below), and if you emailed us, we keep the correspondence for at most 1 year after the matter is closed (privacy policy, section 10). Apart from this we keep nothing linked to the account. If you bought a subscription, it is handled by Google Play or the App Store; you cancel it in the store, deleting the account does not cancel it. We delete the subscription records (RevenueCat, privacy policy section 9) within 30 days of deleting the account; the stores keep their own purchase records under their own rules.

Shared horses: your own horses (created by you) are deleted with all their data, for invited members too. From horses that others shared with you, you only leave: your entries there stay with the horse's owner. Your nickname and membership are deleted, as are any unused invite codes you created.
