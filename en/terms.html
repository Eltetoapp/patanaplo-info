The Patanapló mobile app (Android and iPhone) helps you organise horse care: tasks and reminders, training and health diary, costs and settling up, stock, documents, photos, statistics, PDF summaries, cloud backup, shared horse, features for livery yards.

Technical requirements: Android 7.0 or later, or iOS 15 or later. Cloud backup, shared horses and purchases need an internet connection; the rest of the diary works offline.

The app is a tool: it does not replace the advice of a vet, farrier or other professional. Suggestions in it (e.g. how often to vaccinate or worm) are for general information only.

9. Shared horse, invited members

Members invited to a shared horse (sharer, trainer, family member, the horse's owner at a livery yard) can use the horse for free under the inviting person's plan. The inviting person is responsible for whom they invite. Invited members see the horse's data according to the role chosen by the inviting person (editor or view-only). An editor can add new entries and change or delete their own entries; the horse's owner can change any entry, and sets the team and the cost split. The horse's owner can remove members at any time, and anyone can leave.

Entries and messages written for a shared horse stay with the horse even if someone leaves. If the horse's owner deletes the horse or their account, the horse's data is deleted for all members.

If you record other people's data (e.g. name, phone number), you must make sure you are allowed to do so (e.g. the person knows about it).
