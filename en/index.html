In short

Patanapló is a horse diary app. By default, everything you enter is stored only on your own phone. If you turn on cloud backup (by signing in), a copy of your data is kept in the cloud so that it is still there after you change phones and you can use it on several devices. There are no ads, no tracking, and we do not sell your data. To manage subscriptions, the app uses the RevenueCat service with an anonymous identifier (section 9). Below we describe in detail what happens to your data. If you share a horse with others (shared horse), the invited people can see that horse's data too (section 4).

1. The controller

The controller under this policy is Novák Tuzson Csaba (private individual registered for tax), the developer of the app. In Google Play and the App Store, the app appears under the developer name Eltetoapp.

Contact: kapcsolat.elteto@gmail.com

We are not required to appoint a data protection officer.

2. Data recorded in the app

The data you enter in the app is stored only on your phone: the horse's details and photo, tasks, training sessions, health entries, vaccinations, exams, costs and settling up, feed and stock, the names and phone numbers of team members, grooms and contacts, as well as documents you upload (e.g. a photo or PDF of a horse passport, invoice or contract) and the pictures in the photo diary.

If you do not use cloud backup, this data is not sent to the developer or any third party. In that case the app only sends the anonymous data needed to check your subscription (section 9). If you turn it on, we handle the data as described in section 3.

If you record other people's data (e.g. the name and phone number of a sharer, trainer, groom or vet), please only enter data you are entitled to enter and that the person concerned knows about.

3. Cloud backup and account (if you turn it on)

Using cloud backup is voluntary. You can sign in with a Google account, on iPhone also with an Apple account, or with an email address and password.

  • Data processed: your email address; with Google or Apple sign-in, the name and identifier they provide (with Apple you can choose to share an Apple-generated relay address instead of your real email address); an internal user ID; the sign-in method; the time the account was created and of the last sign-in; all data recorded in the app (listed in section 2, including documents and photos); and the times of backups. With email registration we do not store your password in readable form: the provider only keeps an irreversible fingerprint (hash) of it, which the developer cannot see either.
  • Technical logs: during sign-in and backup, Google (Firebase) may log technical data for a short time for the security of the service (e.g. IP address, time). The developer does not use these for any other purpose.
  • Purpose: backing up your data, restoring it on a new phone, syncing between your devices and, if you initiate it, sharing a horse with others (see section 4).
  • Legal basis: providing the service, i.e. performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Processor: Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase: sign-in, database, file storage). The data is stored on servers in the European Union. Google handles the data as a processor under its own terms and provides standard contractual safeguards.
  • Security: data travels over encrypted connections and is stored encrypted. Access rules ensure that each user can only reach their own data (and data shared with them).
  • Retention: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (cog at the top right → Account and cloud backup → Delete account), we delete your data stored in the cloud immediately; it disappears permanently from the provider's backups within 30 days at the latest.
  • If you sign out, the data in the cloud and the data on your phone both remain.

You can also request account deletion by email at the address below; in that case we delete the account and its data within 30 days. Details: Account and data deletion.

4. Shared horse, stable view and message board (if you use them)

You can share a horse with others (e.g. a sharer, trainer, family member, or at a livery yard the horse's owner). To do this, the horse's owner requests a single-use invite code valid for 7 days and sends it to the invited person themselves. Joining requires signing in (section 3).

  • Data processed: the nickname you give when inviting or joining; which horses you are a member of and in what role (editor or view-only horse owner); the invite code, its expiry and its creator; and the messages you write on the message board (text, time, your name).
  • Who sees it: all data of a shared horse (tasks, diary, health entries, costs, documents, photos, messages) is visible to every member of the horse, together with the list of members and their nicknames. A view-only horse owner also sees everything but can only write messages. Invited people cannot see the owner's other horses.
  • What you write for a shared horse belongs to the horse: if you leave or are removed, your entries stay with the horse and the horse's owner controls them. On the message board you can delete your own messages, and the horse's owner can delete any message.
  • End of membership: you can leave at any time, and the owner can remove anyone. The horse's data is then removed from the former member's phone and their access ends. If the owner deletes the horse or their account, the horse is deleted for everyone. If an invited member deletes their account, they simply leave the shared horses.
  • Legal basis: providing the service (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), initiated by the inviting and the joining user.
  • Retention: the invite code becomes worthless when used or expired, and is deleted when its creator deletes their account; membership and nickname until you leave, are removed or delete your account; messages together with the horse's data, until the owner or their author deletes them.

Staff rota and monthly summary: the names you enter in the staff rota (e.g. grooms' first names) are stored on your phone and, if you have turned on cloud backup, also saved to the cloud as described in section 3. The rota and the monthly summary (PDF) are created on your phone and only reach others if you send them (section 7). Please only enter names you are entitled to enter and that the person concerned knows about.

5. What we do not collect

  • personal identifiers (except if you turn on cloud backup, see section 3, and the anonymous subscription identifier, see section 9),
  • location data (except GPS rides, which you start yourself, see section 6),
  • per-user usage data, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

The app has no ads, no analytics service (such as Google Analytics), no tracking code, and uses no cookies.

Anonymous daily counter: to see how many people use the app and which parts, we count some events as daily totals: for example on how many phones the app was opened that day, how many new horses were added, how many times the plans window was opened, and how many subscriptions or trials were started. Only the day and a number reach our server (Google Firebase, European data centre), without names, email addresses, or account, device or advertising identifiers, so no individual user can be identified from it.

6. Permissions

Notifications: reminders (farrier, vaccination, medication, stock, monthly settling up) are scheduled locally by your phone. For this the app asks for permission to send notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.

Notification of new messages (shared horse): if you are a member of a shared horse and signed in, the app saves your phone's notification identifier (push token) and the app's language to your account. When someone writes on the horse's message board, our server sends a notification to this identifier for the other members of the horse, with the horse's name, the sender's nickname and the beginning of the message, in the language of your app. On Android the notification is delivered by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), on iPhone by Apple (Apple Push Notification service). The server sending the message (Google Cloud Functions) runs in the European Union.

  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), as the notification is part of the shared horse service.
  • Retention: as long as you are signed in and the notification is turned on. When you sign out, delete your account or turn it off (cog at the top right → Notifications → "Notify me about new messages"), we delete the identifier; the server no longer uses an identifier that has not been refreshed for 60 days.
  • Processor: Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase), and on iPhone Apple for delivering the notification.

Photos and files: the app only reads a photo or PDF if you choose it. These stay on the phone and, if you have turned on cloud backup, are also saved to the cloud as described in section 3.

Camera: you can also take a photo with the camera for the horse's picture, the photo diary or a document. The app only uses the camera when you choose to; for this the phone asks for permission, which you can withdraw at any time in the settings. Pictures taken this way are treated the same as chosen photos.

Location (GPS rides): when you record a ride with GPS, the app uses your phone's precise location. Only from the moment you open the "GPS ride" start screen (to search for the GPS signal), and from starting the recording until "Finish"; during this time it also records with the screen locked (on Android a persistent notification, on iPhone the blue indicator at the top of the screen shows this). Otherwise the app does not use location in the background. While recording, the points are stored on your phone. When you save, the ride's route (in simplified form), distance, time, speed and estimated gaits are added to the horse's training diary on your phone; if you have turned on cloud backup, they are also stored in the cloud as described in section 3, and on a shared horse the horse's team can see them. To show the map, the app downloads map tiles from MapTiler; MapTiler can see roughly which area you are viewing from the requested tiles and your IP address, but it does not receive the points of your ride. On the shareable picture, the area around the start and end point (200–400 m) is left out so that the location of your yard is not revealed.

  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), because you start the recording; you can revoke location access in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Retention: until you delete the training entry (or the horse); an unfinished recording is deleted when you discard it.
  • Processors: for the map, MapTiler AG (Switzerland; based on the European Commission's adequacy decision); for cloud backup, Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase).

7. Sharing and exporting

If you share something from the app (for example a PDF report, a monthly summary, the cost spreadsheet (CSV file), a yearly summary picture, a staff rota, an invite code, a calendar file, a document or a reminder message), it goes through your phone's share menu to the app you choose (e.g. email, Messenger, WhatsApp, Google Drive). The data handling of that service then applies; the developer has no access to it.

The "Google Calendar" button opens the Google Calendar website with the task's name and date. This only happens if you press it, and from then on Google's data handling applies.

8. Device backup

Android's own backup (if you have turned it on) may copy your phone's data to your Google account. Google handles this under its own terms; the developer has no access to it. Documents and photo diary pictures are excluded from this backup.

On iPhone, iCloud backup (if you have turned it on) may likewise copy your phone's data to your Apple account. Apple handles this under its own terms; the developer has no access to it.

The app's "Save as text" feature (cog at the top right → Data → Advanced) gives you a text that you save wherever you like.

9. Purchases, subscription

Payment: purchasing a subscription and payment are handled by Google Play (on iPhone by the Apple App Store), under their own privacy terms. The developer does not see your name, card details or address. The stores give the developer aggregated reports (e.g. number of sales per country).

Subscription records (RevenueCat): so that the app knows which plan you have, and so that a purchase can be restored after changing phones or reinstalling, we use the RevenueCat service. The app asks it at every start, even if you have not bought anything (in that case it only learns that you have no subscription).

  • Data processed: an identifier (without sign-in a random, anonymous identifier; when signed in, your internal user ID, see section 3); the plan purchased; the times of purchase, trial, renewal, cancellation and expiry; the price and currency; the store and the store's purchase identifier; the country; the phone model and system version, the app version, and technical connection data (e.g. IP address). It does not receive your name, email address or card details.
  • Purpose: checking the subscription and providing paid features, restoring purchases, checking that the trial is only used once, for a shared horse determining whether the owner has a valid subscription, and handling refunds and complaints.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Processor: RevenueCat, Inc. (1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA). The data is transferred to the United States; the transfer is based on the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission, included in RevenueCat's data processing agreement.
  • Retention: as long as your account or subscription exists. When you delete your account, we also delete your data stored at RevenueCat, within 30 days at the latest. The stores keep their own purchase records under their own rules.

Gift codes: if we give you a gift plan, we use your email address only to activate the gift and for the related correspondence, and delete it at the latest 1 year after the matter is closed. Legal basis: your consent, given by providing the address (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

10. If you email us

If you write to the address above, we process your email address, your name (if you give it) and the content of your message.

  • Purpose: answering your question, handling bug reports.
  • Legal basis: your consent, given by sending the message (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Retention: at most 1 year after the matter is closed, then we delete it.
  • Processor: the correspondence is stored by Google's Gmail service (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland).

We do not sell your data, do not pass it on to third parties, and do not send newsletters.

11. This website

This policy is displayed by the GitHub Pages service (GitHub, Inc., USA). For security reasons GitHub may keep technical logs of visits (e.g. IP address). We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking code on this page.

12. Deleting data

You can delete the data on your phone in the app (cog at the top right → Data → Advanced → "Delete all data and start again"), a single horse together with its data, or by uninstalling the app. You can delete the data stored in the cloud and the account with the Delete account button, or request it by email (details). Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription: that has to be done in the store (Google Play, or on iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions). You can request deletion of our correspondence at the address above.

If you feel we have infringed your rights, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country of residence, or with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH):

14. Children

An account can be created from the age of 16; under 16 with the consent of a parent or guardian. Under 18, a subscription must be purchased by a parent or guardian, or with their approval.

15. Changes

If the app gets a new feature that involves data processing, we update this policy in advance and also let you know in the app. Earlier versions are available on request.
